Miscellaneous Frequent Question and Answers (F&Q)
1. How can I create an account?
Answer: To create an account, begin by navigating to our website and locating the 'Sign Up' button, typically
found in the top right corner of the homepage. Once you click on this button, you will be directed to a
registration form where you will need to provide essential information such as your name, email address, and a
secure password. After entering your details, you may be asked to verify your email address by clicking a
confirmation link sent to your inbox. Once verified, you can complete the registration by following any additional
prompts, such as selecting a username or setting up security questions. After successfully finishing these steps,
your account will be created, and you can begin accessing all the features and services available on the platform
2. What payment methods do you accept?
Answer: We offer a variety of convenient payment options to ensure a smooth transaction process for online

to these payment networks. In addition to card payments, we also accept PayPal, which allows you to make
secure payments directly from your PayPal balance, linked bank account, or associated credit cards.
3. How can I track my order?
Answer: To track your order, simply log into your account using your credentials and go to the 'Order History'
section, typically found in your account dashboard. Once you are in the 'Order History' section, you will be able
to see a list of all your recent purchases. Find the specific order you wish to track, and you will see detailed
information about its status, including a tracking number and a link to the courier’s tracking page. By clicking on
this link, you can view real-time updates on the location and estimated delivery time of your shipment, ensuring
you stay informed about its progress from dispatch to delivery.
4. What is your return policy?
Answer: Our return policy gives you the flexibility to return products within 3 working days of purchase for a full
refund, if the items are in their original, unused condition and packaging. This means that the product should
not show signs of wear or damage and must include all original accessories, documentation, and packaging
materials to be eligible for a return.
5. How to cancel the order?
Answer: To cancel your order from your account, log in and navigate to the ‘Purchase History' section. Find the
order you wish to cancel, and if it has not been shipped yet, you will see an option to cancel the order. Click on
the cancellation option, confirm your request, and the order will be canceled. You will receive a confirmation,
and any payments made will be refunded.
