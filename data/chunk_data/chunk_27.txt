Question: how can i initiate an order return?
Answer: to initiate a return, visit our returns page, where you will find clear, step-by-step instructions on how to proceed. you will need to provide your order details to generate a return shipping label. once the label is generated, pack the item securely in its original packaging and send it to the specified return address. after we receive and inspect the returned item, we will promptly process your refund.
