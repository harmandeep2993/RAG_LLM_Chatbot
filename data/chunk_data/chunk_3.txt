Question: how can i track my order?
Answer: to track your order, simply log into your account using your credentials and go to the 'order history' section, typically found in your account dashboard. once you are in the 'order history' section, you will be able to see a list of all your recent purchases. find the specific order you wish to track, and you will see detailed information about its status, including a tracking number and a link to the courier’s tracking page. by clicking on this link, you can view real-time updates on the location and estimated delivery time of your shipment, ensuring you stay informed about its progress from dispatch to delivery.
